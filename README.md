One line, max 120 characters — the fortune of a houseplant sitting in a pot on the windowsill.

> Your future is bright, like the windowsill at noon. Water yourself, grow slowly, and never trust a cat.

`main.ts` writes that exact line (103 characters, no trailing newline) to `entry.txt` in the output directory.
